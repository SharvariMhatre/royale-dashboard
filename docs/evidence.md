# Claims and Evidence appendix

27 claims from the three analysts. Each lists the table behind it, the result, the alternative explanation we tested and the limitation. Source: `app_data/team/all_claims.parquet` (merge of `p1_claims`, `p2_claims`, `p3_claims`), generated by `src/make_docs.py`. Data release 0.9.0-draft; the data is synthetic. Anomalies are leads to inspect, never accusations.

## Q1

### P2-01: 0 of the manager's bottom 10 are truly bad machines; replacing all 10 is not supported

- **Evidence table:** p2_verdicts
- **Result:** Bad spot: 6; Short exposure: 3; Inspect: 1
- **Alternative tested:** Raw house-win ranking vs luck-, exposure- and location-adjusted ranking
- **Limitation:** Thresholds are judgment calls; see notebook rule table
- **Confidence:** High (from p2)

### P2-02: 9 of the bottom 10 sit in the Back Forty, the weakest zone

- **Evidence table:** p2_zone_index
- **Result:** Back Forty earns 65% of the floor's theo/hour for the same games (Main Entrance: 119%)
- **Alternative tested:** Game mix: index compares each game with the same game floor-wide
- **Limitation:** Zone effect may reflect layout and walking distance, not one cause
- **Confidence:** High (from p2)

### P2-03: Location causes low earnings: machine M-1152 moved Back Forty -> Main Entrance and its theo/hour changed +106%

- **Evidence table:** p2_move_effects
- **Result:** 14.26 -> 29.35 $/h; floor-adjusted +106% over 147 days
- **Alternative tested:** Unmoved machines over the same dates changed -0.2%
- **Limitation:** Only 4 moves in the data; one machine is a small sample
- **Confidence:** Medium (from p2)

### P2-04: Downtime does not explain the bottom 10

- **Evidence table:** p2_machine_summary
- **Result:** Lowest availability on the floor is 98.1%; median 99.7%
- **Alternative tested:** Theo per available hour (removes downtime) gives the same bottom group
- **Limitation:** Availability is meter-based; short outages inside an hour may be missed
- **Confidence:** High (from p2)

### P2-05: Inspection candidates: payout far outside game math beyond luck

- **Evidence table:** p2_inspection_candidates
- **Result:** M-1158 hold 3.0% vs 10.0%, z=-4.57
- **Alternative tested:** Exact per-spin variance from game_math; 10,000 fair simulations; |z|>3.29 so ~0.3 false alarms expected across all machines
- **Limitation:** A lead to inspect, not evidence of wrongdoing
- **Confidence:** High (from p2)

### P2-06: Short time on the floor puts machines in the bottom 10 that should not be judged by totals

- **Evidence table:** p2_verdicts
- **Result:** M-5076: 49 days; M-1120: 132 days; M-1118: 55 days
- **Alternative tested:** Per-available-hour comparison
- **Limitation:** New machines may still be ramping up
- **Confidence:** High (from p2)

### P2-07: Simulation and closed-form luck scores agree

- **Evidence table:** p2_luck_scores
- **Result:** Max difference between simulated luck score and normal CDF of z: 0.012
- **Alternative tested:** Simple luck residual (house win - theo) ranks differently because it ignores game volatility
- **Limitation:** Normal approximation; fine at tens of thousands of spins per machine
- **Confidence:** High (from p2)

### P2-08: Back Forty lost share while its Main Entrance walkway was closed

- **Evidence table:** p2_b40_closure_effect
- **Result:** 1 before closure: 67% of rest of floor; 2 during closure: 46% of rest of floor; 3 after closure: 64% of rest of floor
- **Alternative tested:** Compared with the rest of the floor over the same dates
- **Limitation:** Closure overlaps March seasonality
- **Confidence:** Medium (from p2)

### P2-09: M-1181 was also unlucky: only 0.9% of fair simulations did worse

- **Evidence table:** p2_luck_scores
- **Result:** House win $26,977 vs theo $32,516
- **Alternative tested:** Expected win (theo) per hour is normal for its game and zone
- **Limitation:** Testing 10 machines at 5% gives ~0.5 false positive expected
- **Confidence:** Medium (from p2)

### P2-10: Slot L-38-5 holds 2 of the bottom 10 (M-5076, M-1120): the retired machine was already replaced in the same weak spot

- **Evidence table:** p2_verdicts / bronze_placements
- **Result:** Replacing machines without moving them repeats the problem
- **Alternative tested:** Same game title before and after; per-hour theo compared with zone peers
- **Limitation:** One slot
- **Confidence:** High (from p2)

## Q3

### P1-01: Machines were available 99.62% of scheduled hours. Downtime cost 5,021 machine-hours and about $90,663 of theo (0.39% of all theo).

- **Evidence table:** p1_downtime
- **Result:** 5,021 h; $90,663 lost theo
- **Alternative tested:** Lost theo valued at each machine's own theo per available hour in that month (not floor average), so busy machines count more than quiet ones.
- **Limitation:** Upper bound: some players just move to another machine when one is down. Assumes an outage hour is an average hour.
- **Confidence:** high (from p1)

### P1-02: Repair tickets explain 98.3% of downtime; every downtime hour matches an operations ticket (0 'unexplained' machine-days). 28% of fault downtime is waiting for a technician, not repairing.

- **Evidence table:** p1_downtime_events
- **Result:** 1207 faults; avg wait 1.14 h, avg fix 2.95 h
- **Alternative tested:** Reconciled machine_day (scheduled - available seconds) against ticket intervals split at the 06:00 gaming-day boundary.
- **Limitation:** Ticket open time is when the machine became unavailable; faults nobody noticed would not appear.
- **Confidence:** high (from p1)

### P1-03: Ridgeline machines fail most: 4.04 faults per 100 machine-days (95% CI 3.73-4.34) vs Northwind 1.67.

- **Evidence table:** p1_downtime_summary
- **Result:** 680 faults on 94 machines
- **Alternative tested:** Rates per machine-day on the floor, not raw counts, so a brand with more machines is not penalized.
- **Limitation:** Manufacturer overlaps with game title and age; this is association, not proof the brand causes faults.
- **Confidence:** high (from p1)

### P1-04: Downtime was worst in 2025-05 (1,009 machine-hours, avg wait 2.22 h vs 1.14 h overall) and the grave shift waits 1.96 h for a tech vs 0.90 h on day shift; repair time is the same on every shift.

- **Evidence table:** p1_downtime_summary
- **Result:** grave 1.96 h, swing 0.85 h, day 0.90 h
- **Alternative tested:** Repair (fix) time compared by shift too: it barely changes, so the gap is staffing/response, not harder faults.
- **Limitation:** Shift assigned by ticket open time; long outages span shifts.
- **Confidence:** high (from p1)

### P1-05: Adding one technician (best case: grave shift) would recover about $513 of theo in the 30-day window (range $128-$769) for $11,520. Preventive maintenance: $-125 for $9,000. Downtime is real but too cheap to fix with this budget.

- **Evidence table:** p1_actions
- **Result:** benefit < cost for both downtime actions
- **Alternative tested:** Tried each shift and the worst manufacturer; valued hours with the time-of-day profile of play.
- **Limitation:** Theo is expected win, not profit; ignores guest goodwill from fewer dead machines.
- **Confidence:** medium (from p1)

## Q5

### P1-06: The comp program cost $190,669 (Jan-Jun), already inside the $937,936 POS cost of goods; guests saw $682,750 of menu value.

- **Evidence table:** p1_comps
- **Result:** $190,669 comp cost
- **Alternative tested:** player_period comp cost matches property_day comp cost to the cent; counted once.
- **Limitation:** Only carded play earns points; comp cost is house cost, not menu price.
- **Confidence:** high (from p1)

### P1-07: On 2025-05-01 the point price of comps was cut overnight: 13.1 -> 5.8 points per comp dollar (-56%). Comp cost per $1,000 carded theo rose from $11.95 to $13.09 (95% CI of change +0.65 to +1.67).

- **Evidence table:** p1_comp_rate_daily
- **Result:** comp cost/day $1,027 -> $1,105; redemptions/day 322 -> 340
- **Alternative tested:** 30 days either side only (less seasonality): cost per $1k theo change +0.55 (CI -0.06 to +1.16). Daily block bootstrap by week.
- **Limitation:** No control group: the change was floor-wide, so other May events can't be fully ruled out.
- **Confidence:** high (from p1)

### P1-08: Making comps cost fewer points did not bring more carded play: carded theo per day changed -1.6% (95% CI $-4,009 to $+1,288 per day).

- **Evidence table:** p1_comp_change
- **Result:** $86,022 -> $84,629 carded theo/day
- **Alternative tested:** Checked the 30-day window around the change as well.
- **Limitation:** Two months after; long-run loyalty effects unknown. A randomized pilot would settle it.
- **Confidence:** medium (from p1)

### P1-09: Platinum comp cost per month went from $2,629 to $8,442 while Gold fell $17,203 -> $14,682: more players are reaching Platinum (tiers use lifetime points), so tier mix is a second driver of comp cost.

- **Evidence table:** p1_comps
- **Result:** Base: $10,981 -> $10,586/month; Gold: $17,203 -> $14,682/month; Platinum: $2,629 -> $8,442/month
- **Alternative tested:** Split by tier at month end so the rate change and tier creep are not confused.
- **Limitation:** Tier is the tier after the player's last event that month.
- **Confidence:** medium (from p1)
