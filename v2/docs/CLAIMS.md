## C1 (Q1) None of the GM's 10 is a weak machine: 7 sit in a bad spot, 2 had short time on the floor, 1 needs inspection.

- **Table / query:** `analysis/q1_machines.py: verdict() over floor (machine_day joined to prepared_daily_view, active rows)`
- **Result:** All 310 machines earn at least 0.84x the same game in the same zone (leave-one-out).
- **Alternative tested:** The machines are simply bad games. Tested: compared with the same game in the same zone on the same days, excluding the machine itself.
- **Limitation:** Verdict thresholds (0.8, 0.85, 120 days, |z| > 3.29) are our choices; the dashboard recomputes them for any period.

## C2 (Q1) M-1158 pays more often but smaller: hit rate 38.6% vs 31.5% configured, award per hit 2.50x vs 2.85x for the same game elsewhere. Effective return 97.0% vs 90% configured.

- **Table / query:** `analysis/q1_machines.py: mechanism(); sessions + game_math`
- **Result:** Hit-rate z = 124 in meters and 103 in carded sessions; luck z = -4.57. Present at every bet level, every month, carded (2.4% hold) and uncarded (4.3%).
- **Alternative tested:** Bad luck or a metering fault. Luck: the hit test ignores award size, and z = 124 is not luck. Metering: all 776,297 sessions use legal bets, every payout is a multiple of the game's increment, coin-in = bet x spins on every session.
- **Limitation:** Data can't say why the paytable differs. This is a lead to inspect, never an accusation.

## C3 (Q2) Back Forty's spot is the cause: it earns 0.61x what its game mix earns elsewhere, and fell from 67% to 46% of the rest of the floor while its walkway was closed (Mar 3 to Apr 6), recovering to 64%.

- **Table / query:** `analysis/q2_q3_demand_downtime.py: walkway(); floor_walkways, zone_traffic_hourly`
- **Result:** The closure effect is more extreme than all 78 placebo windows of the same length. Back Forty entries per counter-hour: 25.19 before, 17.62 during.
