# Don't replace the ten. Fix the spot and inspect one machine.

The GM planned to replace the 10 lowest-earning slots (Jan to Jun 2025). We tested luck, location and a leak on every machine.

**Location.** Nine of the ten sit in Back Forty, which earns 0.61x what its games earn elsewhere. When the walkway from Main Entrance closed (Mar 3 to Apr 6), Back Forty fell from 67% to 46% of the floor rate and recovered to 64%. The drop is larger than every one of 78 fake closure windows. A new machine in the same slot (M-1120 to M-5076) earned the same; M-1152 doubled after moving out.

**Leak.** M-1158 pays on 38.6% of spins vs 31.5% configured, with smaller wins (2.50x the bet vs 2.85x), at every bet size, every month, carded or not: an effective 97% return vs 90%, about $200 a day. All 776,297 carded sessions reconcile, so the meters are sound; check the paytable. A lead to inspect, not an accusation.

**Luck.** Closed-form luck scores spread as chance predicts (SD 1.09) and don't persist between quarters (r = 0.08). No machine earns under 84% of the same game in the same zone.

**Other questions.** Downtime is 0.38% of hours (at most $96k of expected win). June demand ran 4.3% below the weekday norm as Las Vegas visitors fell 11%. Tracked players lost $15.6M, within 1% of the math; the top 10% carry 59% of losses, so nothing here targets heavy losers. On May 1 a $1 comp fell from 13.1 to 5.8 points in every tier, lifting comp cost from 0.73% to 0.88% of expected win.

**Model.** Five forecasters were compared by rolling-origin CV on Jan to Apr. Gradient boosting won and was scored once on May to Jun: $52.5k a day of error vs $218k for a constant baseline. Its 80% band covered 75% of days, reported as is. It is the yardstick for July's promotions.

**Plan.** We scored all 530 legal plans with 4,000 simulations each. The best: inspect M-1158 ($1,200), Back Forty signs ($7,500), two promotion weekends ($30,000). Spend $38,700, keep $61,300, expected net +$32k, gain in 94% of runs. The GM's lease plan ranks 466th and loses in every run.

Every number is recomputed in the browser and re-derived by a 40-check script that fails on any mismatch.
